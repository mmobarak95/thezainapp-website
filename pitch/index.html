    05 · Why now
    On air chapter 1 of 3
    Why Now — the case for one intentional life
    The Zain Studio · recorded for you
    Chapter 01 — A coach for the whole life

    A coach who could hold your habits, books, training, and mind in one head used to cost a salary. LLM inference collapsed ~280× in 18 months — from $20 to $0.07 per million tokens. An always-on companion for an intentional life now costs cents per user per week.

    Stanford HAI, AI Index 2025 · Epoch AI
    Chapter 02 — The intentional life, assembled by hand

    Therapy & companionship is the #1 consumer gen-AI use case of 2025; "organize my life" is #2. People are already reaching for a deliberate life — a chat window for the mind, an app per habit, a shelf that never meets the gym — fragments with no memory of each other, or of their actual days.

    HBR / Zao-Sanders, 2025 · Menlo Ventures: general assistants take 81% of consumer AI spend
    Chapter 03 — Intention is what people pay for

    Wellness is a $6.8 trillion economy heading to $9.8T by 2029 — growing faster than global GDP. Health & fitness apps alone took $4.5B in consumer spend last year. What all of it buys, one app at a time, is the same thing — a more intentional life, sold in pieces.

    Global Wellness Institute, 2025 · Sensor Tower

    Three forces, one conclusion — living intentionally just became buildable.
