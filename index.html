Twelve weeks, one dot a day

Every plan I ever made looked like this.

Week one, easy. Week two, mostly. Then one bad week, and nothing after it. A plan gets written on a good day, and life doesn’t only have good days. When my week fell apart, the plan asked for the same thing anyway. So I stopped, the way everyone does.

What Zain does with a week

01

It goes on your days.

Zain puts your goal on your calendar, next to your habits. Each day shows the next small step, and what it’s for. Tick it, and Zain knows.

02

It knows the week you actually had.

Not the runs you planned, the ones you did. Every Monday the note is waiting on your home screen, and it says what it thinks in plain words.

03

It adapts the plan, and the goal is still on track.

When the week changes on a Tuesday, tell Zain what’s going on, in your own words. Your calendar updates with your updated plan.

One place, and a calendar that adapts to your life.

Your habits, your journal, your books and your training live in one app. That is the page the plan reads, so it can see the whole of you and not just the goal.

Why not just a chatbot?

A chat window can write a plan. It can’t see your week.

Zain is with you during your week

You don’t have to explain what happened. The habits you ticked, the runs your watch caught, the ones you skipped. Zain already knows, so you never have to type it in.

It comes to you

Monday morning, the note is waiting on your home screen. Miss two sessions of your plan in one week, and Zain helps you replan the week so you can get back on track.

And the goal is still on track

It will move the week around as much as it needs to. The goal adapts to how your life is going, so it always stays within reach.
